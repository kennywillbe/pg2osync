# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in pg2osync to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and respectful. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal
  attacks are not.
- Assume good faith and ask for clarification before assuming intent.
- Keep discussion focused on the problem, the code, and the evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks,
publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of technical
discussion.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to `conduct@pg2osync.dev`. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour they consider unacceptable, and will explain the reason
for the decision.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
